About us

Engineers who care about healthcare

Nimble IT Solutions is an engineer-led healthcare technology company based in Bedminster, New Jersey, and the maker of Nimble Health Intelligence.

Who we are

We started out as an online-business management studio for coaches and entrepreneurs. Along the way, we found the work that matters most to us: helping independent healthcare practices and the MSOs that support them run like well-equipped organizations without losing what makes them independent.

Our background is engineering, cybersecurity and process automation. We know what keeps a modern practice running, from Microsoft 365 and EMR exports to texting compliance and denial queues. Today we build our own platform, Nimble Health Intelligence, and provide end-to-end IT for physical therapy practices.

We work under Business Associate Agreements with the MSOs and practices we serve, and we treat their patients’ data with the same care they do.

We still offer our original digital business services →

What we believe

  • The practice decides. We recommend and build, and owners and clinicians make the decisions.
  • Security first. Access is limited to what’s needed, every view is logged, and nothing is public by default.
  • Plain language. Clear dashboards, clear pricing, clear answers.
  • You own your data. Always, and in writing.
