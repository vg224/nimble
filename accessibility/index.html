Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Nimble IT Solutions LLC wants the Website and Nimble Health Intelligence to be usable by everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our approach

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. That includes:

  • Semantic page structure and a skip-to-content link
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Sufficient color contrast and readable type sizes
  • Full keyboard navigation, with visible focus indicators
  • Layouts that work on phones, and with browser zoom up to 200%
  • Labels on every form field

Known limitations

Some third-party services we link to, such as Calendly scheduling, are outside our control and may not fully meet these standards. If you have trouble with one, contact us and we’ll help another way.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of our Website or Platform, email vg@nimbleits.com and describe the page and the problem. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
