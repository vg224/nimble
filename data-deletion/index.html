Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains how to ask for information received through Nimble IT Solutions LLC’s Meta (Facebook) integration to be deleted, and what happens when you make a request.

If you submitted a Lead Ads form

When you fill in a lead form on a clinic’s Facebook or Instagram ad, your information is delivered to that clinic’s account in Nimble Health Intelligence. To ask for it to be deleted:

  1. Email vg@nimbleits.com with the subject line “Data deletion request.”
  2. Include the name of the clinic or business whose ad you responded to, and the email address or phone number you entered on the form.
  3. Please don’t include health details. We don’t need them to find your record.

What happens next:

  • We confirm we received your request within 5 business days, and we may ask you to verify your identity.
  • We tell the clinic about your request. The clinic controls your information and must approve deletion, because some records may have to be kept by law (for example, if you became a patient).
  • With the clinic’s approval, we delete your lead information from the Platform within 30 days, and confirm to you by email what was deleted. If the clinic has to keep some records, we will tell you that.

You can also contact the clinic directly. Deleting data from our Platform does not delete information held by Meta. To manage it there, use your Facebook privacy settings.

If you connected a Facebook Page (clinic administrators)

You can stop sharing data at any time in either of these ways:

  • In Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, select the Nimble app and choose Remove.
  • In the Platform: disconnect the Page on the integration settings screen. You can also ask your Nimble contact to do it.

When a Page is disconnected or the app is removed, we stop retrieving new leads right away, and we delete that Page’s access tokens within 30 days. Leads that were already delivered stay in your clinic’s Platform account and follow your agreement with us. To have them deleted as well, email vg@nimbleits.com.

Questions

For anything else about your data, see our Privacy Policy or email vg@nimbleits.com.
