Built for the MSO model

The MSO recommends. The practice decides.

Management services organizations need efficiency and control without crossing into clinical or ownership decisions. Nimble Health Intelligence builds that line into the software.

  • One sign-in across every clinic, with roles assigned per clinic
  • Clinic data separated by design, so owners see only their own practice
  • Approvals go to the practice’s named owners, as set out in your management services agreement
  • Owners can approve from their phone
  • An audit trail of every change and every view of patient data
How it works for MSOs

How a decision flows

MSO prepares→Practice owner approves→Audit log records it

Budgets, vendor contracts, marketing content, write-offs and staffing changes all follow the same path. Clinical matters stay with licensed professionals and outside the platform.
