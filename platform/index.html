The platform

Nimble Health Intelligence

One secure place for the business side of healthcare: analytics, RCM analysis, P&L tracking, CRM and HR, built for MSOs and the independent practices they support.

Book a demoContact us

Analytics & KPI dashboards

Weekly review dashboards for each clinic and across clinics: visits, new patients, cancellations, campaign results and financial KPIs. Ready to print for the weekly meeting.

  • Per-clinic and cross-clinic views
  • Visits, new patients, cancellations and no-shows
  • Campaign and referral results
  • A printable weekly-review pack

RCM analysis

See where revenue is getting stuck. A denials work queue, A/R aging and payer mix, plus write-off and adjustment requests sent to the practice for approval.

  • Denials work queue by reason and payer
  • A/R aging and payer mix trends
  • Write-off and adjustment approvals with thresholds
  • Works alongside your EMR’s billing. Claims stay in the practice’s name and NPI.

P&L tracking

Revenue, expenses and margin by clinic and service line, with budget vs. actual for owner and MSO reviews.

  • Clinic and service-line profitability
  • Budget vs. actual, month by month
  • Consistent reporting across every managed clinic
  • Business data kept apart from patient data

CRM & patient outreach

Branded email and text campaigns that bring patients back, with consent tracking, opt-out handling and an approval step before anything is sent.

  • Reactivation and recall campaigns
  • Consent and opt-out records for email and SMS
  • Online booking and lead capture
  • Locked, branded templates so staff can’t break compliance

HRMS

Hiring and change requests, time-off approvals, policy acknowledgments, HIPAA training tracking and personnel documents. All of it is kept separate from patient data.

  • Hire, change and role-end requests
  • Time-off and schedule approvals
  • Policy acknowledgments and HIPAA training
  • Restricted personnel records

Approvals & governance

One approval engine and audit trail across every module. The MSO prepares and recommends, and the practice’s named owners decide.

  • Approvers set from your management services agreement
  • Delegations, backups and escalation
  • Mobile approvals for owners
  • A complete, append-only audit log
One foundation

Shared by every module

Adding a module or a clinic doesn’t mean another login, another vendor or another place your data lives.

One sign-in

One account per person across every clinic, with authenticator-app MFA and automatic session timeouts.

Roles per clinic

Practice owners, managers, clinical staff and MSO staff each see only what their role allows, at the clinics where they work.

Approval engine

Named approvers, backups and delegations, matched to your agreements, and nobody approves their own request.

Audit log

Changes, decisions and views of patient data are recorded and can’t be edited later.

Reporting layer

Dashboards read from a dedicated reporting store rather than live operational data.

Mobile-ready

It installs on a phone like an app, so owners can approve from anywhere.

Works with what you have

Your EMR stays your system of record

Nimble Health Intelligence doesn’t replace your EMR or billing system. It works alongside them. We import the reports you already run and turn them into dashboards, work queues and approvals. Claims always go out under the practice’s name and NPI.

  • EMR and billing data imports (for example, eClinicalWorks)
  • Website booking forms that feed the CRM
  • Email and text messaging with consent records
  • Microsoft 365 sign-in on our roadmap

Coming next

  • Social media: plan, draft, approve and schedule posts for each clinic, with nothing that identifies a patient.
  • Calls and after-hours: overflow and after-hours call handling with callback queues.
  • Review responses: drafted replies to online reviews that never confirm someone is a patient.
