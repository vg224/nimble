Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nimble IT Solutions LLC (“Nimble,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects information through:

  • our website, nimbleits.com (the “Website”);
  • Nimble Health Intelligence, our platform for healthcare practices and management services organizations (the “Platform”); and
  • our Meta integration, a Meta (Facebook) app that lets our clients connect their Facebook Pages so that leads from their Lead Ads forms are delivered into the Platform (the “Meta Integration”).

1. Our role: who controls your information

Website. For information you give us through the Website, such as a contact form or a request for a demo, Nimble decides how that information is used.

Platform and Meta Integration. We provide the Platform and the Meta Integration to healthcare practices, clinics and management services organizations (our “Clients”). When you submit a Lead Ads form on a Client’s Facebook or Instagram ad, you are sharing your information with that Client. We process it only on the Client’s behalf, under our written agreements with them. Where the information is protected health information under HIPAA, we act as the Client’s business associate under a Business Associate Agreement.

If you submitted a form to a clinic, that clinic’s own privacy notice also applies, and the clinic decides how it follows up with you. We will help the clinic respond to any request you make (see Your choices and rights).

2. Information we collect

Information you give us on the Website

  • Contact form: your name, work email, organization, organization type and message.
  • Scheduling: if you book a call, the booking is handled by Calendly, which collects your name, email and any answers you provide.

Please do not send patient information or other health information through the Website.

Information about Platform users

  • Account details for Client staff: name, work email, role and clinic assignments.
  • Security and audit records: sign-in events, multi-factor authentication status, and a log of actions and record views in the Platform.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting providers receive standard technical information when you visit: IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time. The Website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

3. Information from Meta (Facebook) Lead Ads

When a Client connects a Facebook Page to the Platform, an authorized Page administrator logs in with Facebook and grants the Meta Integration specific permissions. We then receive:

InformationWhy we receive it
The Client’s Page name and ID, and the name and ID of the Page administrator who connected itTo show which Pages are connected, and who connected them
Page access tokensTo retrieve new leads for that Page. Tokens are encrypted and never shown to Platform users.
Lead form submissions: the information you entered (for example, name, email, phone number and answers to the form’s questions), along with the form, ad and campaign identifiers and the time of submissionTo deliver the lead to the Client’s Platform account so the Client can follow up with you

We only retrieve leads from Pages a Client has connected, and only for as long as the Page stays connected. We do not access your Facebook profile, friends, posts or messages through the Meta Integration.

4. How we use information

  • To deliver leads to the Client that ran the ad, so its staff can contact you about your inquiry.
  • To operate, secure, maintain and support the Platform and the Meta Integration.
  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule calls you request through the Website.
  • To keep audit records, prevent fraud and misuse, and meet legal obligations.

What we don’t do. We do not sell personal information. We do not use lead data or other Client data for our own marketing, to build advertising profiles, or to train artificial intelligence models for others. We do not send health information back to Meta or any other advertising platform.

5. How we share information

  • With the Client whose ad or Page you interacted with.
  • With service providers who help us run our services under contracts that protect the information. These include Amazon Web Services (hosting, under a Business Associate Agreement), Formspree (Website contact form) and Calendly (scheduling).
  • For legal reasons, when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our Clients, patients, users or the public.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition. Any successor remains bound by our Client agreements and this policy.

6. How we protect information

The Platform runs on HIPAA-eligible Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States. Safeguards include encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication for every user, role-based permissions checked on every request, web application firewalls, and an audit log of changes and record views. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information against unauthorized access, loss and misuse. See our Security & HIPAA page for more detail.

7. How long we keep information

  • Page access tokens are kept until the Page is disconnected or the app is removed. They are deleted within 30 days after that.
  • Lead data is kept for as long as the Client’s agreement with us requires, and is deleted or returned to the Client when the agreement ends. The Client decides how long it keeps its own patient and prospect records, as required by law.
  • Website inquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and for our ordinary business records.
  • Security and audit logs are kept for the periods required by our security program and applicable law.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including New Jersey and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses. To make a request, email vg@nimbleits.com.

If your request concerns information you submitted to a clinic, we will forward it to that Client and help them respond, because they control that information. You can also contact the clinic directly. See our Data Deletion page for how to ask for Meta lead data to be deleted.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Calls, texts and emails

A Client may contact you by phone or email about the inquiry you submitted. A Client will send you marketing text messages only if you have given the written consent the law requires. Submitting a lead form does not by itself mean you consent to marketing texts. You can reply STOP to any text message, or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, to opt out. Opt-outs are recorded and honored across the Platform.

10. Cookies and third-party services

The Website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Some pages load content from third parties, and these services may receive your IP address and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts (web fonts)
  • Calendly (when you open a scheduling link)
  • Formspree (when you submit the contact form)

The Platform uses strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in securely.

11. Children

Our Website and Platform are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through them. Clients are responsible for complying with the law when their services involve minors.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
