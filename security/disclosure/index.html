Security

Vulnerability Disclosure Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Keeping healthcare data safe is central to what we do. If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in nimbleits.com or Nimble Health Intelligence, please tell us.

How to report

Email vg@nimbleits.com with the subject “Security report.” Please include:

  • a description of the issue and where it is (URL or component);
  • steps to reproduce it, and the potential impact; and
  • how we can reach you.

We confirm receipt within 5 business days and keep you updated as we investigate and fix the issue.

Guidelines

  • Only test against accounts and data you own or are authorized to use.
  • Do not access, change, download or keep patient or client data. If you come across it, stop, and tell us right away.
  • Do not run denial-of-service tests, social engineering, phishing or physical attacks, and do not use automated scanning that degrades service.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.

Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to follow this policy, we will consider your research authorized, work with you to understand and fix the issue, and will not pursue legal action against you for it.

A machine-readable contact is published at /.well-known/security.txt.
