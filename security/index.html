Security & HIPAA

Built to protect patient data from day one

Healthcare data deserves more than a checkbox. Here’s how we protect the information our clients trust us with.

Business Associate Agreements

A BAA at every link in the chain

We sign a BAA with every MSO and practice we serve, and our cloud infrastructure runs under a BAA with Amazon Web Services.

Practice↔MSO↔Nimble IT Solutions↔Amazon Web Services

HIPAA-eligible infrastructure

We use only HIPAA-eligible AWS services, hosted in the United States, with a security baseline that includes threat detection and long-term activity logging.

Encryption everywhere

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with dedicated encryption keys. Databases are private and never exposed to the internet.

Strong sign-in

Every user needs authenticator-app MFA. Sessions time out automatically, and compromised passwords are blocked.

Least-privilege access

Permissions are checked on the server for every request (person, clinic, module and action), and each service uses its own credentials, rotated automatically.

Every view logged

An append-only audit log records changes, approvals and every view of patient data, not just edits.

Protected at the edge

A web application firewall with rate limiting and managed rule sets sits in front of the platform and its sign-in.

Data kept in its lane

Patient data, HR records and business data are kept in separate stores with separate access rules.

Compliant messaging

Email and text outreach respects consent and opt-outs (TCPA and CAN-SPAM), and a person approves every message before it goes out.

Your data, your choice

Clients own their data. Our agreements let an MSO move its environment out of our organization whenever it chooses.

FAQ

Common questions

Will you sign a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. We sign a BAA with every MSO and practice before we handle any protected health information.

Where is our data stored?

In HIPAA-eligible Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States, covered by a BAA with AWS.

Who owns the data?

You do. Our agreements say so, and an MSO can move its environment out of our organization whenever it chooses.

Do we have to replace our EMR or billing system?

No. Nimble Health Intelligence works alongside your EMR and billing. Claims continue to go out under the practice’s name and NPI.

Do you use AI with patient data?

Only through HIPAA-eligible services covered by our BAA, and only where it has a clear purpose. Patient information is never used in public AI tools.

Can we send patient information through this website?

Please don’t. The contact form on this site isn’t meant for health information. Once a BAA is in place, we’ll set up a secure way to share data.

Have a security questionnaire?

We’re happy to walk your compliance team through our controls.
