For MSOs

Manage many practices without blurring the lines

Give your team one place to support every clinic, and give each practice’s owners the visibility and decision rights their agreements require.

  • Cross-clinic dashboards for your weekly and monthly reviews
  • Per-clinic data separation and roles
  • Approval routing that mirrors your management services agreement
  • A repeatable onboarding runbook for each new clinic
  • A BAA chain from practice to MSO to Nimble to our cloud provider
  • Your data and environment stay yours and can be moved if you need to
For independent practices & clinics

Know your numbers and keep your independence

Get the analytics and operating tools larger groups have, without giving up ownership or switching EMRs.

  • Weekly KPIs: visits, new patients, cancellations and collections
  • Denials, A/R aging and payer mix in one view
  • P&L by service line, with budget vs. actual
  • Patient reactivation by email and text, with consent tracking
  • HR approvals, policy acknowledgments and training records
For physical therapy practices

End-to-end IT for PT practices

We cover everything from the front desk computer to the patient booking page, under a BAA and with one team accountable for it.

Microsoft 365 & email

Business email, Teams and SharePoint set up and secured, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC so your messages reach inboxes.

Devices & help desk

Laptops and front-desk PCs managed and kept up to date, plus a real person to call when something breaks.

EMR support & data

EMR reports and exports turned into dashboards and work queues, plus help with vendors and integrations.

Website & online booking

A fast, accessible practice website with a booking request form that captures consent the right way.

Texting & phones

Registered business texting (10DLC) with opt-in and opt-out handling. Business phone setup and call-flow planning.

Security & HIPAA safeguards

MFA everywhere, access reviews, backups, security baselines, and help with policies and staff training.
